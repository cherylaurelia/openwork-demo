# Translation review

1. First line: `TRANSLATION REVIEW · <target language>`.
2. A numbered list, one item per string: the source, the translation, and `OK` or a suggested replacement in backticks.
3. Last line: `Verdict: ship` or `Verdict: fix first`.

Nothing else.
